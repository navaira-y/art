Stories behind the paintings

The artist

Just a kid who still
thinks they can
change the world.

Dana Habayeb paints in acrylic from the UAE.

Pop icons, homeland memory and the desert land on the same deep edge canvas. Banksy's girl with the balloon leads KAWS toward a free Palestine. Pikachu sits in a keffiyeh under the olive trees. A Bedouin lives inside a horse's eye. The work is thick, swirling and personal, made for the moment you recognise yourself inside a picture.

Every original is signed, arrives ready to hang and ships worldwide with a certificate of authenticity.
