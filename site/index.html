The Artist

“I paint the moment you recognise yourself in a picture.”

Dana Habayeb is a young artist painting what we all carry — the icons that raised us, the land we miss, the people who made us. Pikachu rests under olive trees. Heroes laugh inside Van Gogh skies. Dunes run like weekends that never end. Her acrylics are thick, swirling and unapologetically personal — made for that jolt of this is mine, this is me. If your story isn’t on these walls yet, she’ll paint it.
