# 10-second single-take prompt (attach the 15 artwork images + keyframes)

Photorealistic cinematic ONE continuous take, exactly 10 seconds, NO cuts, NO
transitions, NO jumps. A single uninterrupted fast smooth steadicam walk at
human eye height through a rectangular luxury art gallery, moving at an
accelerated brisk glide so the entire hall is covered within 10 seconds.
Camera enters through the open ornate black wrought-iron gate and immediately
speeds forward smoothly. LEFT wall: seven framed pop-art paintings (the
attached images, in the given order) flow past one after another while the
camera faces slightly left. The camera then turns naturally toward the FRONT
wall with one centered framed artwork, sweeps RIGHT around the elegant
light-stone staircase with slim balustrade, and keeps gliding along the RIGHT
wall where the remaining seven framed paintings (attached images, in order)
pass one by one. End still moving, facing the entrance gate. Dark polished
black marble floor with mirror reflections, white walls, warm recessed ceiling
spotlights, realistic frames, natural shadows, believable depth. Fast but
stable motion, no stutter, no warping of the artworks, one unbroken take.
